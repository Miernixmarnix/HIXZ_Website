About Marnix

Your guide to Aruba's best-kept secrets.

The HIXZ Mission

Welcome! I specialize in making hiking and adventure experiences in Aruba fun, safe, and unforgettable for everyone. While most visitors stick to the crowded hotel beaches, I built HIXZ to show you the wild, rugged, and truly majestic side of the island that most people never get to see.

Whether we're navigating the volcanic steps of Hooiberg, exploring the rugged Sasariwichi Dunes, or trekking through Aruba's most hidden coastal trails — my goal is to guide you step by step through the best nature the island has to offer. The best way to see Aruba is on foot, away from the crowds, where the real desert meets the sea.
