Guided Hikes & Filming

I am Marnix van Pelt, your guide to Aruba's best-kept secrets. I lead group hikes through the island's most rugged and majestic trails, specialized in making hiking and swimming adventures in Aruba fun, safe, and unforgettable for everyone. While most visitors stick to the crowded hotel beaches, I built HIXZ to show you the wild desert-meets-the-sea side of the island. But I don't just guide—I film. Using a DJI Osmo Action 5 and professional audio, I capture your entire adventure, providing you with fully edited, color-graded footage ready for TikTok and Instagram.
