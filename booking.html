Aruba Adventures & Filming Hub

I am Marnix van Pelt, your guide to Aruba's best-kept secrets. I lead group hikes through the island's most rugged and majestic trails, specializing in making hiking and swimming adventures fun, safe, and unforgettable for everyone.

While most visitors stick to the crowded hotel beaches, I built HIXZ to show you the wild desert-meets-the-sea side of the island. But I don't just guide—I film.

Using a DJI Osmo Action 5 and professional audio, I capture your entire adventure, providing you with fully edited, color-graded footage ready for TikTok and Instagram. Leave the island with an amazing experience and the exact content you need to remember it by.

TRAIL FUN FACTS: NATURAL BRIDGE TO CONCHI

Want to know what you're getting into? Here is some history on this specific route:

  • Blackstone Beach: This is the only beach in Aruba entirely made of black volcanic stones instead of white sand.
  • The Original Bridge: The famous Natural Bridge collapsed in 2005, but the rugged windward coast remains completely untouched by commercial development.
  • Conchi (Natural Pool): Protected by a wall of volcanic rock, this hidden pool requires a serious trek through Arikok National Park to access safely.
